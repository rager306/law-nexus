## Verdict

The immutable lexical evidence, grammar, context and binding separation is sound. M203 completion is promotion-control completion, not legal readiness. R035/R070 and N2 remain HOLD. The principal failure is measuring generated artifacts instead of independently established semantics, combined with faulty context eligibility. Pullenti's Decree, DecreePart and DecreeChange mechanisms are useful functional prior art; its general SemanticService is not a proven normative interpreter. No code, dictionaries, generated tables or numeric thresholds are adopted.

## Findings

### RC28-F01 — C5 admission and measurements are not derived from coding
Severity: critical. Class: evidence.
`crates/ln-consultant-parser/src/bin/npa-gold-ladder.rs:162-186,324-337` assigns DoubleCodedAccepted, alpha=1.0 and perfect layer counts without consuming independent coder units. Proxy/non-claim labels do not reconcile positive machine fields. Require not-measured outcomes, typed publication validation, coder-bound computed agreement, pre-adjudication scores and append-only superseding artifacts. Perfect agreement is legitimate only when computed; missing or undefined agreement must not become 1.0. Reproduce by removing one coder envelope and asserting publication refusal.

### RC28-F02 — Sampling and metadata do not implement stated strata
Severity: high. Class: evidence.
`npa-gold-ladder.rs:24-42,109-140` derives year from arbitrary path digits, type from the full path including law-nexus, and selects sorted prefixes without using the advertised seed. Use validated metadata or Unknown, seeded content-hash ordering and Work/edition-family separation. C2/C5 overlap is not inherently invalid when declared development reuse; it is not independent evaluation. Preserve C3 isolation, distinguish active-learning discovery from evaluation, and justify provider quotas.

### RC28-F03 — C4 identity and comparability are insufficient
Severity: high. Class: evidence.
`crates/ln-consultant-parser/src/contour_diagnostics.rs` contains a constant diagnostic corpus fingerprint and proxy stage counters. `m203-s09-c4-operational-receipt.json` records fail-stale, exit 5, 949s, but does not expose complete argv. Preserve that negative historical outcome. Define actual inventory digests, stage-specific observables, canonical semantic payload comparison separate from operational envelope, complete argv and policy/toolchain binding. Missing output, incomparable inputs and semantic drift must be distinguishable. Fresh execution belongs in a >=3600s operational lane, not a 600s capped check.

### RC28-F04 — UAT acceptance changed across attempts
Severity: high. Class: process.
Local S08 attempt-1 left a live C4 check incomplete; attempt-2 checked the saved fail-stale artifact alongside other runtime checks. This is a criterion-equivalence concern, not proof of an automatically illegal PASS: mode policy requires at least one runtime check. Version acceptance contracts and record authorized supersession; receipt presence is not baseline freshness. Do not reset retry counters to conceal history or rewrite prior attempts.

### RC28-F05 — GSD coordination failures are independent of parser semantics

No upstream publication without separate user confirmation.

### RC28-F06 — Document containers are pushed without sibling closure
Severity: high. Class: implementation.
`crates/ln-decode/src/document_context.rs:380-409` takes the current stack top as parent and pushes every container. Consecutive articles can become nested. Reproduce two sibling articles, chapters and independent annexes. Close containers using the document-group structural profile, not one universal hardcoded legal ladder; ambiguous roles stay explicit. Verify parentage and all downstream owner/alias scopes.

### RC28-F07 — Continuation eligibility is global and compares local offsets
Severity: high. Class: implementation.
`document_context.rs:1198-1234` selects other-block act frames, compares fragment-local offsets and creates conflicting continuation edges from multiplicity. Unrelated references are not competing authorized interpretations. Require open grammatical coordination, source block order, owner and boundary compatibility, bounded hops and retained derivation paths before conflict arbitration. No cross-block TextAnchor; three unrelated citations must create no continuation edges.

### RC28-F08 — ThisRef evidence is lost and context ownership is duplicated
Severity: high. Class: implementation.
`document_context.rs:873-877` dispatches CurrentDocumentRequisites with empty ThisRefGrammarEvidence. Compare this path with `crates/ln-temporal/src/document_context.rs`; eliminate divergent authorization semantics while respecting crate ownership. Positive self-reference and negative ordinary cited-act cases are both required. Constant abstention proves neither useful completeness nor correct resolution.

### RC28-F09 — Local grammar cannot depend only on already captured members
Severity: high. Class: implementation.
`crates/ln-decode/src/local_grammar.rs` groups headless captures by optional head and searches backward for a head; it must not join independent sentences or inherit across an unproven boundary. Build local grammatical alternatives from lexical/morphological evidence before final mention arbitration, preserve direct and grammar origins, and retain competing interpretations. Add incomplete-tail, mixed-act and sentence-boundary tests. D388 pair policies remain stopped until individually adopted.

### RC28-F10 — Token and range variants diverge across stages
Severity: medium. Class: implementation.
`lawref.rs` range capture and local grammar differ on dash variants and marker inflection. Introduce shared normalization with original source anchors, optional typed morphology and explicit unavailable states. A written range, endpoint pair and full target membership are distinct. Resolve complete range membership against the ordered structure of the selected edition; never enumerate decimal designations arithmetically or copy Pullenti's threshold.

### RC28-F11 — Semantic cues are substrings and Actor can cover a whole block
Severity: high. Class: implementation.
`crates/ln-temporal/src/semantic_scope.rs:114-242` uses match_indices, allowing temporal 'до' inside 'должен'; a frame can label the whole block Actor. Use token-bound multiword matching, scoped negation and source-backed participant spans. Test 'не обязан', 'обязан не', 'не вправе', and temporal phrases separately. Cue recognition is not a NormRule, and fixes must not activate deferred legal IR.

### RC28-F12 — Self, antecedent, alias and edition reference are different constructions
Severity: high. Class: design.
Preserve separate authorization for current-document self-reference, an earlier cited act, an explicitly declared scoped alias, and a coordinating head. Test declare/use/shadow/conflict and boundary crossing. Preserve 'в ред.' as a source-backed relation candidate without substituting it for Expression identity, edition_date, CTV or force. Existing anchors: `npa-document-context.yaml`, `current-document-requisites.yaml`, ADR-0016/0019/0028.

### RC28-F13 — Amendment syntax is the missing bridge to the temporal compiler
Severity: high. Class: design.
Local Pullenti `sdk/Pullenti/Ner/Decree/DecreeChangeReferent.cs:16-57` represents OWNER, KIND, CHILD, VALUE, PARAM and LOCVALUE; `DecreeAnalyzer.cs:1263-1440` processes nested changes. This is more than amends-document classification. Law-nexus needs independently specified source-level operation candidates for replace/new wording/insert/remove/repeal, nested target scope and quoted operands. Adopt owning contracts first. Extracted operation is not admitted MicroOperation, LegislativeEffect or InForce. Keep Remove, repeal and repeal-of-amendment distinctions; no automatic operation reinterpretation on missing operands.

### RC28-F14 — Pullenti general semantics is not a normative authority
Severity: medium. Class: design.

Use Decree/DecreePart/DecreeChange as functional prior art, not a SemanticService port. Morph alternatives, local composition and scoped coreference are useful concepts; global mutable token embedding, heuristic entity merge, dictionaries and initialization coupling are not adopted. Any differential run is optional and license-gated; agreement with Pullenti is not gold or a second human coder.

## Recommended program and acceptance

Sequence: evidence/control honesty; contract reconciliation and authorization boundaries; lexical/grammar/structural corrections; context/identity/cue integration; independently accepted annotation and operational measurements; amendment syntax to bounded temporal replay; scoped registry and temporal coverage; separately gated normative semantics. Process repair is independent work, not a license to relax product acceptance.

Every implementation finding needs a reproducer, smallest contour that can lie, a fail-closed test and a real composition test. Add metamorphic checks: irrelevant-block insertion does not change a local binding; source mentions survive binding changes (INV-06); each derivation is anchored (INV-08); deterministic replay (INV-11); no completeness from counts/hash alone (INV-22). Deferred adoption gates are stopping conditions, not TODO runtime fields. Review disposition, delivery completion and capability promotion remain distinct.
