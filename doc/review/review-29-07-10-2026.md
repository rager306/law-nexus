## Withdrawn or narrowed earlier claims

- Hold/TextOnly constrain claims, not necessarily retained data; full projection retention is not a bug.
- Complete with zero members is not inherently a defect: processing completeness is not semantic completeness.
- Historical receipts need not equal current HEAD; historical proof must not be presented as fresh validation.
- Hashes establish integrity, not authenticity or legal truth.
- Missing Python parity is not a defect. Payload retention is an explicit Rust choice.
- JSON-LD does not inherently break byte hashes, offsets, strict validation or streaming. No JSON-LD migration is planned here.
